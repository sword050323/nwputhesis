\begin{abstract}
    深空场景三维重建与新视角合成旨在利用有限多视角图像恢复深空地表或空间目标的三维场景表示，并在未观测视角下生成连续、稳定的渲染图像。该任务对于空间探测数据可视化、任务规划、环境理解和虚拟仿真具有重要意义。然而，与常规地面场景相比，深空场景通常存在观测视角有限、成像距离远、远近深度跨度大、地表纹理弱以及光照条件不稳定等问题。这些因素削弱了多视角图像中的几何约束和光度一致性，从而限制了传统三维重建方法与原始3D Gaussian Splatting在远区域细节恢复、高斯基元形态稳定及新视角渲染质量等方面的表现。

    针对上述问题，本文提出一种面向深空场景的Deep-Space Scene Gaussian Splatting（DSS-GS）方法。该方法以3D Gaussian Splatting为基础，构建显式三维高斯场景表示，并通过可微渲染完成场景优化与新视角合成。为适应深空场景中的远区域弱监督、高斯形态不稳定以及跨视角光度不一致问题，本文在原始3DGS框架中引入远区域增强策略、高斯形态稳定约束和可选的参考引导外观一致性模块，使模型能够在保持整体渲染质量的同时，更充分地表达远处地形区域和弱纹理结构，并缓解光照扰动导致的跨视角色彩偏差。
        
    本文的主要工作和成果如下：

    （1）将一种基于深度先验的远区域增强策略引入3DGS。FAME利用辅助单目深度构建自适应远区域掩膜，从而增强远距离地形区域在图像域中的监督信号，缓解远区域在优化过程中容易监督不足的问题。

    （2）提出一种高斯形态稳定化模块，用于约束显式三维高斯基元。RVS结合有效秩正则和体积正则，对高斯形态分布和空间覆盖范围进行约束，从而提升高斯基元在有限视角和弱纹理条件下的表示稳定性。

    （3）设计了一种基于参考引导的外观一致性可选模块，作为跨视角光度不一致条件下的辅助处理路径，用于建模视角间亮度和色彩差异，缓解光照扰动对新视角合成结果的影响。

    （4）在M3arsSynth火星地表序列上的实验结果表明，DSS-GS取得了0.961的SSIM、38.49 dB的PSNR和0.078的LPIPS。与3DGS基线相比，DSS-GS的SSIM提升0.030，PSNR提升2.32 dB，LPIPS降低0.053，说明本文方法能够有效提升深空地表场景的新视角合成质量。

    \keywordslist{三维重建, 新视角合成, 3D Gaussian Splatting, 深空场景}
\end{abstract}

% 英文摘要
\begin{engabstract}
    Three-dimensional reconstruction and novel view synthesis for deep-space scenes aim to recover a 3D scene representation of planetary surfaces or space targets from sparse multi-view images and to render visually consistent and stable images from unobserved viewpoints. This task is important for space exploration data visualization, mission planning, environment understanding, and virtual simulation. Compared with conventional terrestrial scenes, however, deep-space scenes usually exhibit limited view coverage, long-range imaging, large near-far depth variations, weak surface texture, and unstable illumination. These factors weaken multi-view geometric constraints and photometric consistency, limiting the ability of conventional 3D reconstruction methods and vanilla 3D Gaussian Splatting to recover distant details, maintain stable Gaussian primitives, and synthesize high-quality novel views.

    To address these challenges, this thesis presents Deep-Space Scene Gaussian Splatting (DSS-GS). Built upon 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS), the proposed method constructs an explicit 3D Gaussian scene representation and optimizes it through differentiable rendering for novel view synthesis. To handle weak supervision in distant regions, unstable Gaussian shapes, and cross-view photometric inconsistency, DSS-GS incorporates far-region enhancement, Gaussian shape stabilization, and an optional reference-guided appearance-consistency module into the vanilla 3DGS pipeline. This enables the model to better represent distant terrain and weakly textured structures while preserving the overall rendering quality and mitigating cross-view color shifts caused by illumination perturbations.
        
    Our main contributions are summarized as follows:

    (1) A depth-prior-based far-region enhancement strategy is introduced into 3DGS. FAME uses auxiliary monocular depth to construct an adaptive far-region mask, thereby increasing image-domain supervision for distant terrain regions that are prone to under-optimization.

    (2) A Gaussian shape stabilization module is proposed to regularize explicit 3D Gaussian primitives. RVS combines effective-rank regularization with volume regularization to constrain the shape distribution and spatial support of Gaussian primitives, improving representation stability under limited viewpoints and weak texture.

    (3) An optional reference-guided appearance consistency module is designed as an auxiliary path for cross-view photometric inconsistency. It models view-dependent brightness and color variations to reduce illumination-induced artifacts in novel view synthesis.

    (4) Experiments on Martian surface sequences from M3arsSynth show that DSS-GS achieves an SSIM of 0.961, a PSNR of 38.49 dB, and an LPIPS of 0.078. Compared with the 3DGS baseline, DSS-GS improves SSIM by 0.030 and PSNR by 2.32 dB, while reducing LPIPS by 0.053, demonstrating its effectiveness in improving novel view synthesis quality for deep-space surface scenes.

    \engkeywordslist{3D reconstruction, novel view synthesis, 3D Gaussian Splatting, deep-space scene}
\end{engabstract}
