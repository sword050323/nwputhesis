% 中文摘要
\begin{abstract}
    深空场景通常存在观测视角有限、成像距离较远、纹理信息不足、空间结构复杂和光照不稳定等问题。这些因素会削弱图像中用于三维恢复和视角推断的有效信息，使场景几何结构、尺度关系和外观变化难以充分表达。传统多视图几何方法依赖稳定的特征匹配和充足的视角约束，在低纹理、强阴影和视角覆盖不足条件下容易出现重建不完整、结构表达不稳定等问题，进而影响新视角合成质量。因此，如何在有限且非理想的图像观测条件下构建稳定可靠的三维场景表示，并实现质量稳定的新视角合成，是深空环境感知与可视化中的关键问题。

    本文提出了一种Deep-Space Scene Gaussian Splatting (DSS-GS)方法，用于深空场景的三维重建与新视角合成。该方法以3D Gaussian Splatting为基础，利用多视角图像和相机参数构建显式三维高斯场景表示，并通过可微分渲染完成场景优化与新视角渲染。针对远区域纹理弱、场景尺度变化大以及跨视角光照和外观不一致等问题，DSS-GS设计了Far-region Adaptive Mask Enhancement (FAME)远区域增强模块和Rank-Volume Stabilizer (RVS)高斯形态与尺度稳定模块，并在存在明显光度不一致时引入参考引导的外观建模策略作为可选处理路径，以提升模型在非理想观测条件下的适应能力。
        
    本文实现了数据处理、训练、渲染与评估等模块，并在M3arsSynth数据集的火星场景上进行实验验证。结果表明，相比原始3DGS，DSS-GS将SSIM从0.931提升至0.961，PSNR从36.17 dB提升至38.49 dB，LPIPS从0.131降低至0.078。实验结果说明，该方法能够提升深空场景的新视角合成质量，并在点云覆盖和跨视角重投影一致性分析中表现出更稳定的三维场景表示。

    \keywordslist{三维重建, 新视角合成, 3D Gaussian Splatting, 深空场景}
\end{abstract}

% 英文摘要
\begin{engabstract}
    Deep-space scenes are commonly characterized by limited view coverage, long-range imaging, insufficient texture, complex spatial structures, and unstable illumination. These factors weaken the visual cues available for 3D reconstruction and view inference, making it difficult to sufficiently represent scene geometry, scale relationships, and appearance variations. Conventional multi-view geometry methods rely on reliable feature matching and sufficient view constraints. Under low-texture regions, strong shadows, and insufficient view coverage, they are prone to incomplete reconstruction and unstable structural representation, which further degrades the quality of novel view synthesis (NVS). Therefore, constructing a stable and reliable 3D scene representation from limited and non-ideal image observations and producing stable NVS results remain key problems in deep-space scene perception and visualization.

    This paper presents Deep-Space Scene Gaussian Splatting (DSS-GS) for 3D reconstruction and NVS of deep-space scenes. Built upon 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS), DSS-GS takes multi-view images and camera parameters as input, constructs an explicit 3D Gaussian scene representation, and performs scene optimization and novel-view rendering through differentiable rendering. To address weak textures in distant regions, large scene-scale variations, and cross-view illumination inconsistencies, DSS-GS introduces Far-region Adaptive Mask Enhancement (FAME) for far-region enhancement and Rank-Volume Stabilizer (RVS) for Gaussian shape-scale stabilization. In addition, a reference-guided appearance modeling strategy is used as an optional processing path when strong photometric inconsistency exists.
        
    The proposed system implements data processing, training, rendering, and evaluation modules, and is validated on Martian scenes from the M3arsSynth dataset. Experimental results show that, compared with vanilla 3DGS, DSS-GS improves SSIM from 0.931 to 0.961 and PSNR from 36.17 dB to 38.49 dB, while reducing LPIPS from 0.131 to 0.078. The results indicate that the proposed method improves NVS quality and shows a more stable 3D scene representation in point-cloud coverage and cross-view reprojection consistency analysis.

    \engkeywordslist{3D reconstruction, novel view synthesis, 3D Gaussian Splatting, deep-space scene}
\end{engabstract}
